About Me

Capturing Life's Beautiful Moments

Scharles Leendon D. Ebitner

Photographer & Videographer

19 years old, from Botolan, Zambales. A passionate photographer on a journey to turn my love for capturing moments into a lifelong career. As a student, I'm constantly learning, experimenting, and refining my skills to bring stories to life through my lens.

Photography, for me, is more than just taking pictures—it's about freezing emotions, documenting memories, and seeing the world from unique perspectives. Whether it's portraits, landscapes, or street photography, I strive to create images that evoke feelings and inspire others.

This journey isn't just about mastering the craft but also about embracing creativity, connecting with people, and pushing boundaries. Every click of the shutter is a step closer to my dreams, and I can't wait to see where this path takes me.

Thanks for being part of my story. Let's create something unforgettable together!
